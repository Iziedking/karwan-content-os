# Karwan product canon

Karwan is a settlement and credit layer for cross-border SME trade. It runs on Arc Testnet (chain 5042002), where USDC is the gas token. Money sits in escrow contracts, not with Karwan.

Use the repository's published canon for capability claims. Present-tense claims must be backed by a shipped entry. Anything marked not-live or stale-check is not available today. Do not imply mainnet, real-money risk, guaranteed financing, or unsupported integrations.
