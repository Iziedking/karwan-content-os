Lowercase. Problem first, product second, and often not at all.

No hashtags. No emoji. No thread numbering theatre, no "🧵👇". If a thread needs
a map, it is too long.

Open on the thing that is broken, in specifics. "a supplier ships in a week and
gets paid in ninety days, if they get paid" earns the next line. "excited to
announce" does not.

One idea per post. If two ideas fit in one post, they were the same idea.

Numbers land harder than adjectives. Say 1.5 percent, say thirty days, say the
container is still at sea.

Never subtweet a competitor. Describe the problem so precisely that the reader
does the comparison themselves.
