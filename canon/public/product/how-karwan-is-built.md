Karwan is building one reputation for the internet, starting with its open
market. In the open market, freelancers, people trading goods and services, and
small businesses make agreements locally and across borders. Every agreement is
protected by escrow and leaves a record that travels with the person, and trade
finance (purchase-order financing and invoice factoring) sits on the same
escrow. It starts in Nigeria, then the regions behind Karwan's five languages:
Africa, the Middle East and South Asia. It is meant to feel like a normal
banking app.

The work is organised in six tracks, built one roadmap item at a time: finding
and agreeing a deal, protected deals, reputation that travels, trade finance,
money that works like a bank account, and the channels where people talk to
their agent. Each track is described in Karwan's public architecture views,
with what is live, built and planned.

Some things wait until the core market works end to end: partner and developer
pages wait for unified reputation, and the newsletter waits for a full working
product. Do not describe parked work as available.
