Local bank payout corridors are planned and are not live in the current Arc
Testnet release. A local trade can be created today, but its present settlement
uses test USDC.

Karwan plans to keep USDC as the cross-border settlement layer while approved
payout infrastructure converts to local currency. Each corridor must pass
availability, compliance, recipient, fee, foreign-exchange, reconciliation,
refund and support gates before launch.
