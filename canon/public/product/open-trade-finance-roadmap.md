A broader quote market for eligible Karwan-originated receivables and purchase
orders is planned. Invoice factoring, purchase-order financing and the financier
desk exist in the current testnet build, while the broader open market does not.

The supplier will compare the advance, repayment, spread, expiry, recourse and
concentration controls, then choose whether to accept. Karwan does not finance
arbitrary outside invoices and does not guarantee an advance.
