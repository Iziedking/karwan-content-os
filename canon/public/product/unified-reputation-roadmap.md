Karwan is building one reputation that travels with a person across platforms.
This is the direction the rest of the product serves, and it is not live in the
current Arc Testnet release.

People join a new marketplace, job board or region and vet themselves from zero
every time, because their record is split across platforms that do not talk to
each other. Unified reputation brings that record together: easy to track, easy
to look up, hard to fake. It starts with Karwan's market, whose completed
deals are the first and most heavily weighted input.

Agents will work only with data a person already owns, after they connect a
source and agree. Nothing is leaked, sold or scraped, and consent can be
withdrawn. Other platforms join as partners once the working model is designed
with them; none is named until an agreement exists. The reputation layer is
being built on Circle's agent stack, while escrow, staking and Circle's payment
tools bring the market on chain.

Today a person's Karwan reputation comes only from completed Karwan deals.
