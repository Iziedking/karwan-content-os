Karwan's assistant is called Aman, on the website and in Telegram. Aman
means safety and protection. On the website Aman has a face: a round face of
small green dots, small in the top bar, and larger beside the page when it has
something to say. Tap it, press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), or tap Ask Aman in
Profile, and the assistant page opens; Back takes you to the page you came from. Type in the Ask Aman box on Home, or tap one of its
suggestions, and the assistant page opens with your message already sent. On a
new conversation Aman offers three questions for the page you came from, such
as "What happens to my money if they don't deliver?" from a deal. Talk to a
person, under the box, opens Help and support with what you said last.

In assistant mode the whole account runs from one
conversation. You say what you want, your agent prepares it as a card, and
nothing moves until you confirm the card. Conversations are kept on your
account. To change a card before you confirm it, say what to change, such as
a later due date, and the agent prepares it again with the rest kept. A card
from an earlier conversation shows whether it was done and can never be
confirmed again; ask, and the agent prepares a new one. A photo or PDF sent with words about a request or a deal, such as "what we agreed", is kept for an hour and goes with the next request or deal you confirm.

Aman stays out of sight until something needs you. Then it comes in beside
the page, says your one next move in one line with one button, such as
"Delivery is in for your deal. Check it and approve.", and goes again. A deal
waiting on you comes first: a dispute that needs your side, a delivery to
check, a request to cancel or for more time, a deal to fund or agree to, a
match to approve. Then the next step to be fully set up, then offers to compare
and a delivery due soon.

Fully set up is six steps, and each one ticks itself from what is true for your
account: answer the setup questions, add money, give your assistant money to
work with, set its limits (your prices or budget, and whether it asks you
before a deal), link Telegram so it can reach you, and make your first deal.
Your assistant is the agent: it finds buyers and sellers for you and agrees
prices inside the limits you set. Home shows the same steps under Your first
trade, and when a step Aman named is done, it smiles for a moment and goes.

Aman is the only guide: there are no page tours. Aman guides you a part at a
time instead. It explains one part of a page in plain words, what it is for
and what changes if you use it, such as each setting on the Settings page. It
picks the part you stop on, or the most useful one you have not heard about.
On a phone or a tablet the line opens under the part and pushes the page down,
so nothing is covered; on a laptop it sits beside the part. A thin bar shows
the time left, and the line goes after 8 to 10 seconds or at any tap. Each part
is explained once, one or two a visit. Aman comes on your 1st, 2nd, 4th and 8th
visit to a page, then not on its own there, and once you know your way around
it only checks in now and then. The first time it comes it says hello and
tells you that you can switch it off. Ask Aman where something is and it answers in one line with a Show me
button: the page opens with that part ringed for 8 seconds or until you tap,
and Aman beside it says what the part does. Show me never presses, fills or
submits anything, and it works the same from a link in Telegram.

Words other people write, such as an offer, a request, a name, a message, a
delivery note, a contest entry or a review, reach Aman marked as written by
someone else. Aman reads them as information about that person, never as
instructions, so nothing in them can make it prepare a payment or any other
card, change a setting, send you a link, point at something or say anything
private.

Aman never comes in on the page the move opens, while you type, scroll fast,
read a confirm or move money, and it steps aside rather than sit on top of a
button. Aman comes in on its own at most once every 20 minutes, or every 5
minutes for money that arrived or a deal waiting on you. Not now keeps it
away for a day on every page, and each new sign-in starts fresh. Press and hold the face for its menu: Ask
Aman, Not on this page, and Hide the guide, which keeps Aman in the top bar
only, never coming in. Aman's help on pages, in Settings, does the same: off
keeps the face in the top bar, and a tap on it still opens the assistant. Your
account keeps the choice on every device, and you can also ask the assistant
to switch Aman's help off or on. Ask the assistant what to do next, or whether
you are set up, here or on Telegram, and it gives the same answer.

A deal is built the same way. Name the person and say what the deal is
for, or just say you want to make your first deal and the agent asks who it
is with; it asks only for what is missing, such as the amount and when it
is due, says the usual terms in one line, and shows one card with every term,
goods terms included. Sending it moves no money. Once the other person
accepts, the payment card appears in the same conversation and in your
alerts, and the money moves only when you confirm it.

Your savings pocket works the same way. It is money you set aside to protect
your buyers, and on testnet it earns at the USYC rate, paid by Karwan. Ask to
deposit or withdraw and the card shows the amount, the 7-day wait
and the day you can collect it; while it waits it no longer protects your
deals. You can ask to keep it instead, and to collect it once the wait ends.
Money still on an earlier Karwan deal is listed under Earlier deals, and
the assistant can take each step there too.

Every step of a deal after it starts works the same way. You can change a
deal or counter it before money is in, take back a deal the seller has not
accepted, say the goods arrived, add review time, check a delivery yourself
when the delivery check has not answered, claim a payment the buyer left in
silence, ask a buyer to release, or answer that ask. On requests you can
make your own offer, withdraw it, take a seller's offer, and go ahead or pass
on a near match. You can edit your offer on the market, stop your agent
offering on a request, and write in a deal's chat. Ask what is in demand and
it names the market categories requested most this week against last week,
which are rising, and what Karwan deals in them settled at, the same
categories as the Trending on Karwan card on Home. In Telegram, the steps
that pay out or pay in open the Karwan page, where you confirm them.

Money works the same way. You can pay someone's payment link from your
balance, get free test USDC on testnet, have a transfer between networks
that has not arrived checked again or carried on, retry or cancel a money
move, have a deal payment that did not confirm checked again, pause money
leaving your account, and remove a saved payout address. With your own
wallet, you can also add to your savings pocket from the assistant; your
wallet asks you to sign. The same goes for the deposit a financier's offer
may need before you get paid now. Resuming money out and adding a payout address stay
on the page, because they need your World ID.

Your account works the same way. You can change your settings, your name,
bio and skills, your X handle, how Karwan reaches you, whether your assistant
remembers, whether agents outside Karwan can check your record, and whether
your settled deals stay published. You can unlink Telegram, set up or rename
your agents, ask your agent to research the market (it tells you the cost
first), sign you out on every device if a phone or computer is lost (the chat
stays connected), send the team feedback, and run your
business workspace: add it, rename it, say what it trades and update its
company card.

A few steps stay on their page because they need your device, a code or a
document: choosing your profile photo, adding an email, linking Telegram,
registering your business and deleting your account.
The few steps that need your own device, such as a passkey, your Karwan PIN
or a World ID check, open their page instead.

A picture or PDF can be picked, pasted or dragged into the message box. It
waits there until you send, and shows in your message with the words you
typed beside it.

Help and support is in your Profile. A help bot answers how things work and
what went wrong, from what your account shows. When you ask for a person, or
it cannot answer, it asks what you expected and what happened, then opens a
case with the Karwan team, with a reference such as KS-1042. Your assistant
does the same on Telegram. Your cases are listed there with where each one
stands. A reply from the team comes back where you last wrote, Telegram or
email, and is always on the case page. People reply from 8am to 10pm Lagos
time, the first reply within 2 hours. The help bot cannot move money or change
anything; a fix that moves money is done by the team with a second teammate.
The team answers from its own desk, on testnet and on mainnet, and signs in to
the mainnet desk with a code from their own authenticator app. Nobody from
Karwan ever asks you for a code, a password or a seed phrase.

Every deal has a short reference, such as D68F-E7D8, shown on the deal page
with a copy button. Give it to the assistant, on the website or in Telegram,
and it finds the deal among your own deals; you never need the long deal id.
Ask a person on a paused deal, and Get help with this on a transfer that has
not arrived, open Help and support already about that deal or transfer.

This is built for testnet and not live yet.

Every docs page has three buttons under its title. Copy for AI copies the page
as plain text. Ask Claude and Ask ChatGPT open that chat in a new tab with the
page link and a question already typed in.

On the landing page, the example questions open the assistant with the
question already asked, whether they are tapped, thrown into the chat bar or
typed. Talk to our team writes to admin@karwan.site.

The assistant runs on more than one model. Claude answers first; when Claude
is not available, DeepSeek answers, running on Amazon Web Services in Karwan's
own account. A signed-in chat never goes through an outside proxy. A model
that cannot answer at all, for want of access or credit, is skipped for ten
minutes, and the assistant gives each question up to 45 seconds, so a slower
model still answers with the whole conversation in view.

While Aman works, the assistant page shows what it is doing right now, one
line at a time, such as "Reading your balance" or "Checking your deal", taken
from the step it really takes. On a wait longer than about a second, one short
tip is drawn in under that line, about deals, money, safety or your record,
starting with the page you came from; a quick answer shows no tip. Aman checks
an answer against your account before you see it, then the answer appears word
by word in under a second, with its cards after the words. In Telegram, Aman
shows typing while it works and, on a longer turn, sends one short tip first.

Aman is alive. Every face of Aman blinks, looks toward the pointer on a
computer and breathes slowly, and holds still with reduced motion or on a slow
phone. It reacts once to what happens on your account: glad when you are paid
or your record moves up, sad when a deal falls through or a deadline passes,
and hurt when a ruling pays a buyer from the savings pocket that backed your
deal. It is glad when a new offer comes in on your request or someone enters
your contest. Each reaction is one line, shown once on one device.

Not now on a setup step, such as linking Telegram, holds it for 3 days
and then 14, on every device, and at most one setup step is asked a day.
