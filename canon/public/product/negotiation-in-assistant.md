Post what you need in the assistant, on the website or Telegram, and you can
close the chat. Your agent asks sellers for offers and negotiates, and the
assistant writes back in the same conversation, and on Telegram, only when
something moves: offers in, your shortlist ready with the seller the agent
recommends, a near match when nothing fits your budget, an agreed deal, the
money locked, the work delivered.

When you post, you can turn on Agree for me. Your agent then agrees with the
seller it recommends, within your budget, and you wake up to an agreed deal
ready for one tap to lock the money. It never agrees above your budget and
never moves money on its own.

Offers move the way a market does. Change your request and your agent looks
again at once at the offers it holds; it can recommend one up to the most you
allowed above your budget, and counters it toward your budget. Sellers who
offered get a turn to keep, lower or withdraw their offer, and sellers whose
offer is not the lowest hear once that a lower one is in, never its price.
Offers only go down, at most three times per seller per request. Sellers never
see how far above your budget you would go.

When your budget is far under what the work goes for and no seller takes it,
your agent tells you plainly, with the market price, and you choose to allow
more or post again. Nobody is pushed below their own price. A seller whose
range a request falls outside, but whose work fits it, hears about it once by
email, Telegram and in the app, and decides.

This is built for testnet and not live yet.
