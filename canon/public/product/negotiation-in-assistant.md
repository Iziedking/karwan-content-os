Post what you need in the assistant, on the website or Telegram, and you can
close the chat. Your agent asks sellers for offers and negotiates, and the
assistant writes back in the same conversation, and on Telegram, only when
something moves: offers in, your shortlist ready with the seller the agent
recommends, a near match when nothing fits your budget, an agreed deal, the
money locked, the work delivered.

When you post, you can turn on Agree for me. Your agent then agrees with the
seller it recommends, within your budget, and you wake up to an agreed deal
ready for one tap to lock the money. It never agrees above your budget and
never moves money on its own.

Offers move the way a market does. Change your request and your agent looks
again at once at the offers it holds; it can recommend one up to the most you
allowed above your budget, and counters it toward your budget. Sellers who
offered get a turn to keep, lower or withdraw their offer, and sellers whose
offer is not the lowest hear once that a lower one is in, never its price.
Offers only go down, at most three times per seller per request. Sellers never
see how far above your budget you would go.

When your budget is far under what the work goes for and no seller takes it,
your agent tells you plainly, with the market price, and you choose to allow
more or post again. Nobody is pushed below their own price. Agents never go
quiet. Every seller whose agent judges that your request fits what they sell
makes an offer. A seller whose lowest price is above your budget still offers,
at their lowest, and you see that offer as outside your request; you decide
whether to take it. A request due sooner than a seller needs, or of a kind they
have not set up yet, and a counter under their lowest price, become one
question to that seller, in the app, the assistant, by email and on Telegram,
with a suggested answer they take, change or turn down. While they decide, you
see that the seller is checking, never their price or limit. Nothing is
offered or agreed while a question waits.

Your shortlist lists every seller who fits and offered, not only the first
few. It ranks them by how well they fit your request, then by reputation and
score, then by how close their terms are to what you asked. The seller your
agent recommends is always within your budget. An offer above your budget is
marked outside your request, and you can still choose it. A seller whose agent
is not linked to their account yet is listed, and you can choose them once
they link it; ads work the same way, so a seller's posted offer reaches you
even when its price is above your budget.

Your request page says how many sellers fit, how many offered and how many are
checking the price, never who. When a minute passes and nobody on Karwan sells
it yet, it says so plainly, and you can share the request with a seller you
know while your agent keeps looking. Sellers who fit hear of your request when it is
posted, not on a timer, and a seller who sets up a new way is matched against
the open requests straight away. Example stock on the market never counts as a
seller.

The agents haggle like people. Neither opens at its limit. A seller's agent
comes down by a schedule, firm while there is time and little competition,
steadier near the deadline or with several offers, one step only after your
agent moved, never under the seller's lowest, and it offers more time before it
reaches that lowest. It aims to agree within three exchanges and never runs
past five; the fourth is a last price, and anything past it is a question to
the seller. Every move carries one short reason you can read, and a reason
never names a limit, a budget or another offer. A seller's agent agrees to a
price under its own offer only when the seller chose Agree for me; otherwise
it asks them first.

This is built for testnet and not live yet.
