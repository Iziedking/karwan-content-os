A user-invoked browser companion for starting a protected trade beside a
supported website is planned and is not live in the current Arc Testnet release.

Trade Anywhere will create a user-approved trade draft and secure invite, with X
as the first focused surface. The counterparty will be able to review, counter or
accept through normal Karwan web without installing the companion.

It will not scrape private messages, inject social actions, post, follow, like,
reply or send messages for the user.
