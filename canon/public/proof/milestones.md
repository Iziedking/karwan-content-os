Karwan is built by Karwan Technologies Limited, a company registered in
Nigeria. The team started building in May 2026.

Karwan won first place in Track 2, Best SME Workflow and Trade Finance, of the
Ignyte and Circle Stablecoin Commerce Stack Challenge 2026. The prize was 5,000
USDC. Karwan presented on the demo day at the Dubai FinTech Summit on
3 November 2026.

Karwan also won Best Chainlink-Powered Upgrade from Chainlink Labs at ETHOnline
2026, a 500 USDC award, for checking deal evidence with the Chainlink Runtime
Environment.

Karwan runs on Arc Testnet today. On Arc mainnet, the reputation and business
registry contracts are live, and the wallet app is open by invitation. Trading
and escrow on mainnet open after a closed beta and after the security and
operational checks pass.

These are awards and milestones, not revenue or customer numbers. When someone
asks about traction, give the awards and where Karwan runs today. Testnet deals
are tests, not income, and Karwan does not quote waitlist or sign-up numbers.
