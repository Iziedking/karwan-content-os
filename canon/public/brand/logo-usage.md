Two assets: a wordmark and a mark, each in a light and a dark variant, shipped as
both SVG and PNG. Prefer the SVG anywhere it will render.

Pick the variant by the surface behind it, not by taste. The dark wordmark goes
on light surfaces, the light wordmark on dark. Never place either on the accent.

Do not recolour, rotate, outline, add a gradient, or set it against a busy
photograph. Give it clear space on all sides equal to the height of the mark.

Community use is welcome under these rules. What is not welcome is any use that
implies Karwan endorses a product, or any placement that could read as Karwan
having issued a statement it did not.
