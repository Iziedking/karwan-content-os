Six rules, in order of how often they get broken.

**Editorial, not dashboard-y.** Karwan is a working desk, but the register is
closer to a financial-times editorial layout than a SaaS admin panel. Generous
whitespace, monumental headings, monospaced metadata.

**Two-tier hierarchy.** Display for intent, in caps with tight tracking. Mono for
system metadata, uppercase and bracketed. Body text stays quiet.

**One accent, used sparingly.** Lime is reserved for the primary call to action,
a LIVE status, positive value movement, and one focal indicator per screen. Never
decorate with it.

**Motion is identity.** Every transition has intent. Nothing snaps.

**No default component-library feel.** If a component looks like an unmodified
default, rounded cards and generic muted descriptions, it is wrong. Re-skin every
primitive.

**Domain semantics over generic labels.** Not "Item 1". `[:MILESTONE 01]`,
`[:ESCROW]`, `[:SETTLE]`, `[:DISPUTE]`. Trade vocabulary is part of the design.
