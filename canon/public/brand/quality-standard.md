Every Karwan design holds to one bar: posts, pictures, screens and flows. The
reference is the November new month post, a green felt calendar on near black:
https://karwan.site/brand/karwan-quality-reference.png

Look at it before starting, and hold the finished work next to it.

**What makes it good.** One hero: one object says one thing and everything else
steps back. Size is confidence: the key figure is far bigger than anything near
it. Real material and one light: fine grain, physical edges, stacked depth, one
light from the top end falling away across the surface. Lime as a surface: one
large confident thing with ink on it, not small pieces scattered around. Our own
shape: hero surfaces are the Karwan tile, with its bigger corner at the bottom
start. Quiet dark and few words: near black, a lot of empty space.

**The palette as materials.**

```
hero surface        #AFC95B    Karwan green with grain and light, one per screen
print on the hero   #16202A    ink, 8.9 to 1 on the green, the largest type
ground              #0F1012    near black, rising to #1F2123 where the light pools
logo and headlines  #F4F4F1    on the dark ground
hardware            #0E0E0E    matte black with one soft highlight
```

**On a screen.** One hero per screen: on Money the amount, on a deal the next
move. A green hero counts as the screen's one accent, so there is no second lime
button. Facts stay in hairline rows. Shadows fall one way and stay soft. Big
figures are weight 600, tightly tracked, tabular, with the currency written out.

**In a flow.** One object carries through: the card you start with is the card you
finish with, the amount becoming the confirm and then the receipt. One move per
step. Things settle with an ease-out and never bounce. A confirm is only as tall
as what it asks. Reduced motion is respected.

**Pictures.** Real objects lit and rendered with real type, never generated by an
image model and never stock.

**What stays the same.** Trust comes first. Calm, money as the loudest thing,
sentence case, honest states, contrast, Arabic and the five languages all still
apply. Decorative gradient fills, orbs, neon or coloured glows, shimmer and emoji
stay out.
