These are the published values, from the product's brand palette.

```
accent              #AFC95B    Karwan green: the one primary action, positive money state
ink / dark          #16202A    ink and dark surface
canvas              #EEF2F7    light theme canvas
paper               #FFFFFF    light cards and email
dark inset          #27313A
text sub on light   #646C74
text sub on dark    #A0A6AD
green text on light #42560F    lime-family text that stays readable on light
hairline on dark    rgba(206, 220, 232, 0.18)
```

The accent is a muted lime, softened so green fills do not glare and dark text
stays legible on top. Use it for one thing per screen. Status colours are
separate and always paired with words.
